# Standard 1.2.0 working designs

The designs from which the 1.2.0 changes are implemented (round 3 in
`tasks/todo.md`). Each workstream's design was reviewed adversarially,
revised, and reconciled with the others; `designs/FOUNDATION.md` lists what
lands first and `designs/RECONCILIATION.md` the conflicts resolved. The
accepted decisions are recorded in RFC-0005 to RFC-0010 under `spec/`; these
files are the working material behind them.

`open-findings-audit.json` is the per-finding audit of the 38 findings that
were open after round 2: what existed, what was normative, what could be done
without an RFC.
