## Deviations

- The TypeScript binding-fixture runner is the package's
  `packages/acs-core/test/bindings.test.ts` (the checks are the package's
  `checkBindings`), not `examples/standard_agents/test/bindings.test.ts`.
- `load_bindings(records, nodes, requires, ontology_version, known=None)`
  takes the ontology's nodes and `requires` pairs and the layer's ontology
  version, rather than a supported major, so V109 uses the core's
  `check_cited_versions`.
- I-JSON integers: |n| <= 2^53 is accepted (the reference executor's limit);
  the design's test named 2^53 as failing.
- A capability string that does not match the id pattern is V101 (unknown
  capability), not S303: the ontology decides what a capability is.
- §5.18.1's table gained `standard` and `destination` rows (RECONCILIATION
  C25, S5's destination policy), and V109 covers a cited `standard`.
- SECURITY.md §5.4's `persist` secret row says the command hook's scan is
  S4's; until S4 lands the prompt hook is what checks it.
- `schemas/interop/standard_agents_mapping.yaml` moved to 1.2.0 / 2.2.0 and
  `tests/test_standard_agents_interop.py` changed: the conventions must name
  the versions the binding implements (`packages/acs-core/src/ontology.gen.ts`),
  and the row-level mapping's versions (1.1.0 / 2.1.0, a dated record) must be
  of the same major and not newer. The index is not rewritten.
- `index.yaml`'s status counts for the ACS findings are left for phase 5
  (other streams change statuses too).
- To meet the figure-gap limit without exemptions, §5.18's lists of
  conditions and codes are tables (§5.18.1's members as Field | Type |
  Required | Description, `capability` "No" with "required unless `control`
  is true"; §5.18.2's offered-tool checks; §5.18.4's composite codes;
  §5.18.5's edge types; §5.18.6's None valid and Rollback; §5.18.8's refusal
  codes). The rules are unchanged. The site draws them (CODE_TABLE_FIGURES,
  `table-5.18.1`), and adds figures for §3 (the design principles) and §10.4
  (the safety-layer fixtures); a slot at a chapter heading now titles its
  figure "from §3", not "from §3.".

## Review round 1

Each issue was verified against the code before it was fixed; every fix has
a test that fails without it (checked by reverting the fix).

1. **(high) Grounded MCP guard refuses control-only tools (R407).** Real:
   `create_grounded_mcp_server` wrapped every `SdkMcpTool`, and the
   PreToolUse hook gives a control-only call no token. Fixed at the root: a
   tool whose loaded mapping is `control` is served unwrapped
   (`grounded_agency/mcp.py`). Tests: `tests/test_mcp_integration.py`
   `TestGroundedServerGuard` (runs after PreToolUse, runs with none, and a
   bound tool still runs once per decision and R407 otherwise; there was no
   test of the guard at all before).
2. **(medium) Binding does not refuse non-I-JSON arguments of a gated call
   with F502.** Real: the digest's error reached the R407 catch, and
   canonical JSON had no integer limit. Fixed: `groundedTool` checks the
   arguments of a call that needs approval with the package's one I-JSON
   check (`iJsonProblem`, new `packages/acs-core/src/ijson.ts`, shared with
   `output.ts`) and refuses F502 before anything is asked or used; R407
   stays for store failures and for the arguments of a call that needs no
   approval. Tests: `grounded.test.ts` (lone surrogate, 2^60), Python
   `test_tool_call_approval.py::test_arguments_that_are_not_i_json_are_f502`
   (and the 2^53 limit), and the call fixture `approval_args_not_i_json`
   (args `{"n": 2^60}`, F502, checkpoint kept), which both runners play.
   `standard_agents.md` documents F502.
3. **(medium) §10.4 omits §5.18.10; header overstates.** Real. §10.4 gains
   "applies the capability checks of §5.18.10" and the statement names any
   §5.18.10 check not applied; RFC-0005 9.1 says the same. Neither layer
   applies the `persist`/`audit` secret rows or the `send` destination row:
   the statement schema gains `not_applied`, both statements list the two
   rows, and the header claims class SL "except the secret and destination
   checks of §5.18.10". Dependency for S4 (secrets) and S5 (destinations)
   recorded in FOUNDATION-notes.md: each removes its line and narrows the
   header when its layers apply the row.
4. **(low) Binding records lack `recorded_by`; nested F504 drops the
   capability.** Real. The nested F504 record and the R407 check-failure
   record now carry `recorded_by` (the composite's, passed into the nesting
   state with its clock); the nested refusal names the invoked tool's
   capability from a registry of the names grounded and control tools state
   (`BOUND_NAMES`; `control: true` for a control tool; none for an unknown
   or ambiguous name, an unbound tool). `TraceEntry.recorded_by` documents
   that only the audit hook's entry for a call no grounded tool recorded
   has none. Tests: `nesting.test.ts` (e), `grounded.test.ts` (R407 and
   F502 entries).
5. **(low) `location.field` uses `/`.** Real. Both loaders write
   `approval.min_approvers`, `approval.expires`, `flags.risk`, and W602
   names `flags.<flag>`. Tests updated in `tests/test_tool_bindings.py`,
   `tests/test_tool_call_approval.py`, and `packages/acs-core/test/bindings.test.ts`
   (with a W602 field test).
6. **(low) Findings resolutions inaccurate.** Real. ACS-01 says "layer
   scope" and that SL requires §5.18.10; ACS-39 and ACS-55 describe the
   fixed behaviour (control tool unguarded, `recorded_by` on every entry a
   grounded or control tool writes); counts are 26 and 21; ACS-02, ACS-03,
   ACS-38 and ACS-41 mention the review's fixes.
7. **(low) Statement schema `expires` accepts any string; `max_approvers`
   unenforced.** Real. `expires` is a duration or null again (the binding
   statement says null: a policy without `expires_ms` sets none);
   `MAX_APPROVERS = 1000` in `approval.ts`, which `policyProblems` enforces.
   `calls.test.ts` validates the statement against the schema and compares
   it with `LAYER_NAME`, `STANDARD_VERSION`, `ONTOLOGY_VERSION`,
   `CHECKPOINT_MAX_AGE_MS` and `MAX_APPROVERS`; the Python test also checks
   `max_approvers`, `roles` and `expires`.
8. **(high) `git stash apply <rev>` is `rollback` for any merge commit.**
   Real (git applies any merge commit as a stash). Now `git stash pop|apply
   <commit>` is `rollback` only when `<commit>` is the full id of a commit a
   checkpoint recorded: the mapper asks `is_checkpoint_commit`, which the
   adapter sets to `CheckpointTracker.has_commit(commit, since=start)`; the
   plugin hook reads the `commit` of the marker and of the markers under
   `used/`. A bare `pop`/`apply`, any other revision, an abbreviated id or
   an option is a gated `mutate`. §5.18.3's table gains the row "Restores a
   checkpoint taken in the scope, which the call names: `rollback`; any
   other restore is `mutate`". Tests: `tests/test_bash_classification.py`
   (mapper and adapter), `tests/test_pretooluse_hooks.py`. Docs: SDK guide,
   hooks README, SECURITY.md, the rollback skill, CHANGELOG. The call
   fixtures play bindings, not a shell classifier, so no fixture can express
   this; the rule is in §5.18.3 and in both language-specific suites.
9. **(medium) `has_valid_checkpoint()` broke `ensure_checkpoint_before_step`.**
   Real. New `CheckpointTracker.valid_checkpoint_id()` (the checkpoint
   `validate_and_reserve` would hold, without holding it); the engine
   returns it, or takes a new checkpoint when there is none. Test:
   `test_the_engine_reuses_a_valid_checkpoint_from_the_history`. CHANGELOG
   Upgrading states the new meaning of `has_valid_checkpoint()` and the
   engine's behaviour.
10. **(medium) A non-I-JSON `x-` member makes every call R407.** Real. Both
    loaders refuse a record with a member that has no I-JSON form (S303,
    the member's name as `field`; the tool stays unbound). The adapter
    computes `config_sha256` once per change of settings or bindings (the
    mapper's `records_revision`) and stores a deep copy of each record.
    Tests: `TestRecordedBy` (the Read call is still decided; the digest is
    computed once per change; the stored record is a copy), parse cases in
    both languages, binding fixture `vendor_member_not_i_json`.
11. **(medium) Output codec disagrees on exponent-form numbers.** Real.
    TypeScript decode checks integer literals in the text only (`scan`) and
    no longer limits parsed values; encode refuses a number it would write
    as an integer literal beyond ±2^53 (2^53 < |v| < 1e21). Shared vectors
    `tests/conformance/output/vectors.json` (25 decode, 11 encode), read in
    place by `tests/test_capability_output.py` and
    `packages/acs-core/test/output.test.ts`; cases where the serializers
    differ (1.8e16) are left out of `encode` and tested per language.
    CONFORMANCE.md lists the file.
12. **(medium) `expiry_minutes` not enforced for tracker-API checkpoints.**
    Real. `CheckpointTracker(max_age_minutes=...)` (the adapter passes
    `expiry_minutes`) caps the default and any explicit expiry, and
    validity also checks `created_at + max_age` (state read back from disk
    included). The F501 message and docstrings point to
    `adapter.create_checkpoint()`. Tests: `TestMaximumAge`.
13. **(low) CHANGELOG lacks Upgrading notes.** Real. Added: the
    `require_layer=True` default and `require_layer=False`; `can_reserve`
    and `release_reservation_in_scope` removed (`resolve_holds_in_scope`);
    `DEFAULT_RESERVATION_SECONDS`; `has_valid_checkpoint`; the engine;
    `max_age_minutes`; the git stash rule; binding records (I-JSON, 1.0,
    dotted fields); the plugin marker's times; the TypeScript codec and
    binding changes; the statement schema.
14. **(low) Hook: overflowing `expires_at` fails open; no 30-minute cap.**
    Real. Times must be whole seconds of at most 12 digits, `created_at`
    is required and not in the future, `expires_at` at most 1800 s after
    it, compared with `(( ))`; otherwise F501 and the marker is removed.
    Tests: `tests/test_hooks_unit.py`
    `test_exit_2_marker_with_impossible_times` (six cases) and the exactly
    30 minutes case.
15. **(low) `min_approvers: 1.0`.** Real. Python accepts a float with no
    fractional part, as JSON Schema's `integer` does (TypeScript already
    did). Tests: `test_min_approvers_written_as_a_whole_float_reads` and the
    binding fixture `approval_integral_float`.

Site: the new §5.18.3 row put §5.18.3 at 1818px between figures on
`/spec/1.2.0/` (limit 1800). Restructured, no exemption: the row is one
